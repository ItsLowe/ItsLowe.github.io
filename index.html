01 · MSc dissertation · 2010–2025

ATP Tennis Forecasting

This study asks whether pre-match ranking, form, surface, workload and matchup history can narrow the gap between TrueSkill Through Time (TTT) forecasts and bookmaker probabilities. It uses chronological model selection and daily rolling-origin testing.

On the 7,725-match test set, the final dissertation reports primary-model log loss of 0.6169 for logistic regression and 0.6159 for the neural network, compared with 0.6576 for TTT and 0.5866 for bookmakers. Section 4.3 reports that the primary models closed 57.3% and 58.8% of the overall TTT–bookmaker log-loss gap. Among the 1,496 test matches where TTT and bookmakers predicted different winners, Section 4.5 reports gap reductions of 69.6% and 75.1%, respectively.

Source note: figures are transcribed from the final submitted dissertation; models were not rerun. The displayed log losses and stated gap are rounded, so they do not reproduce every printed gap percentage exactly when recalculated from rounded values.

03 · Bayesian modelling · 1886–2021

Bayesian Chess Performance

A Gaussian Bayesian hierarchical regression models log Average Centipawn Loss (ACPL), with a player random intercept and terms for year, colour and a post-1996 trend. The original formula, priors, four chains, 4,000 iterations per chain, 1,000 warm-up iterations and seed 123 were preserved in a genuine RStan fit.

Diagnostics were reassuring (maximum Rhat 1.0013, minimum bulk ESS 2,560, zero divergent transitions). Most source-reported summaries were reproduced closely. One interval endpoint differs: the post-1996 step's reproduced upper 95% credible bound is 0.353246, versus 0.3431 in the coursework (about 3.16 quantile MCSE).

Evidence status: Numerically reproduced with documented discrepancies. The coursework does not specify the forecast reference for the Black indicator; black = 0.5 is one plausible midpoint scenario, not a recovered convention. The displayed player-effects image remains an original coursework figure, not output from the reproduced fit.

  • Partial pooling
  • Posterior uncertainty

04 · Student innovation project · 2024

Visualization Design of Foreign Trade Data for Hainan Free Trade Port

Role: Data Curation and Analysis

A team project presenting Hainan foreign-trade statistics from the 2019–2023 provincial statistical yearbooks. The dashboard explores annual import and export trends, trade types, partner regions and product categories. It also includes simple five-year forecasts based on linear regression fitted to the five years of data.

The project presentation describes a Vue 3 and Vite interface with ECharts, alongside Python and scikit-learn for the forecast example. The forecast is exploratory; no accuracy validation is reported here.

05 · Visual GIS case study · Dublin

Green Space Accessibility

This QGIS case study combines a selected street network, public green-space polygons and Census 2022 Small Areas to examine network-based 5- and 10-minute accessibility approximations and green-space supply per resident.

The 360 m and 720 m thresholds assume a walking speed of 1.2 m/s. Buffered service lines approximate accessibility; they are not validated door-to-park isochrones.

Interpretation: the reported 10-minute overlay intersects 2,180 Small Areas whose full recorded populations sum to 532,949. This is not a measured count of residents with 10-minute access. The original GIS workflow was not independently reproduced.

  • QGIS
  • Network analysis
  • Small Area population
